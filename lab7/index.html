5. Temporal Network Questions

1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

Overall connectivity fluctuates from day to day, but the network generally becomes more connected toward the end of the 60-day period. During the first 20 days, there are about 5.5 active links and 8.6 active companies per day on average. During the last 20 days, this increases to about 7.3 links and 9.7 active companies. The most connected days include Days 45, 51, and 57, when all 12 companies are active and 10 links are present.

2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

Fusion Electronics is one of the most commercially active companies across the full period and is especially prominent during the middle portion of the timeline. Later in the period, Ion Systems and Cedar Retail become much more active. Ion Systems has the highest average transaction volume during Days 41–60, while Cedar Retail also develops more connections during this period. The changing node sizes make these shifts in activity visible.

3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

Yes. Several days contain multiple disconnected groups rather than one highly connected network. This is especially noticeable on lower-connectivity days such as Days 3, 4, 9, and 16, when the active network is divided into as many as four separate components. Similar fragmentation also appears around Days 28 and 40. On more active days, larger connected groups form as additional commercial relationships appear.

4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

Several relationships recur frequently, including Delta Components–Fusion Electronics, Apex Textiles–BlueRiver Logistics, and Ion Systems–Lumen Distribution. Later in the 60-day period, new strong relationships appear, such as Fusion Electronics–Ion Systems, Cedar Retail–Keystone Materials, Cedar Retail–Granite Wholesale, and Apex Textiles–Ion Systems. In contrast, relationships such as Harbor Shipping–Keystone Materials and BlueRiver Logistics–Delta Components are active earlier but disappear later.

5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

The network becomes somewhat more cross-regional over time. Cross-regional transactions account for about 64% of transaction records in the first 20 days and about 72% in the final 20 days. The share of total transaction value involving companies from different regions also increases substantially in the later period. This suggests that the commercial network gradually becomes more geographically interconnected.
