5. Design Reflection

I extended my Lab 9 GDP choropleth and Dorling cartogram by adding voice interaction through the browser Speech Recognition API. Users can say commands such as “Highlight China,” “Highlight Japan,” or “Reset.” The interaction supports country selection, which can be difficult when a country is geographically small or when users are viewing the visualization from a distance. Voice is appropriate because the country name naturally specifies the target while the system immediately highlights the same economy in both views. The interface also displays what the system heard and what action was performed, so recognition errors are visible. Speech recognition can fail because of browser compatibility, pronunciation, accents, microphone quality, or background noise. Therefore, microphone access begins only after explicit user action, and conventional mouse clicking and reset buttons remain available as fallback controls.
