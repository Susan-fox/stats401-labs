6. Design Description

The DKU Undergraduate Bulletin was divided into paragraphs and short policy blocks while preserving chapter, section, subsection, and page metadata. During preprocessing, duplicated, very short, and malformed passages were removed. Because PDF text extraction occasionally identifies individual course titles or course-table headings as sections, a cleaned formal-section field was also created for aggregate comparisons while the original hierarchy metadata was retained.

Semantic embeddings were generated with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 sentence transformer. The resulting 384-dimensional vectors were projected to two dimensions using UMAP with 15 neighbors, a minimum distance of 0.15, and cosine distance. KMeans with eight clusters was applied to the original embedding vectors. Topic labels were assigned by examining characteristic TF-IDF terms and representative passages.

In the semantic map, position represents the UMAP projection, color represents semantic topic, and point size represents passage length. Search, formal-section filtering, topic filtering, zoom, and pan support exploration. Clicking a passage displays its metadata, original text, and five nearest semantic neighbors. The Topic × Section matrix summarizes the relationship between formal document organization and semantic structure. Coordinated highlighting connects the two views: selecting a passage highlights its matrix cell, while selecting a matrix cell highlights matching passages in the map.
