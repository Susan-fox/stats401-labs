Lab 2: Four-Dimensional City Visualization

This visualization represents population, temperature, development level, and region for twelve cities.

Design Justification

I used bar height to represent population because height makes quantitative differences between cities easy to compare. Region is represented with color because it is a nominal variable with four distinct categories: North, South, East, and West. Development level is represented by bar width, with Low shown as narrower bars, Medium as medium-width bars, and High as wider bars. This preserves the ordered nature of the variable. Average temperature is represented by the size of the circle above each bar, so warmer cities have larger circles and cooler cities have smaller circles. City names are shown along the horizontal axis, and tooltips provide the exact values for each observation. I hope viewers can quickly compare population while also noticing regional patterns, development levels, and differences in temperature across the twelve cities.
